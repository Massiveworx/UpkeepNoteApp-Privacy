UpKeepNote Privacy Statement

About UpKeepNote

UpKeepNote is a maintenance schedule and reminder utility provided by Massiveworx. Last updated: November 8, 2026.

Your information

The app stores maintenance item names, categories, locations, filter sizes or model numbers, notes, intervals, due dates, reminder choices, and completion history in a local app file. These are used to manage your schedule and calculate the next due date.

Collection and sharing

This version has no account, cloud sync, advertising, third-party analytics SDK, AI service, or external backend. Your maintenance records are not sent to Massiveworx. Massiveworx does not sell or share the maintenance content stored by the app. Recommended-check source links open external websites. Those websites may receive connection information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

With your permission, UpKeepNote gives Apple's on-device notification system the dates and generic reminder text needed to display local alerts. Notification text does not include your item names, notes, or locations. Alerts may appear on the Lock Screen or connected devices according to your Apple settings. Reminders are not remote push messages from Massiveworx. Permission can be changed in system Settings.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete an item or clear all data. Deleting an item deletes its completion history and cancels its pending reminders. Clear All Data removes all locally saved items, history, and pending/delivered app notifications after confirmation. Notification permission is controlled separately by iOS. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading may retain it. Device or computer backup copies may remain under your backup settings.

Apple services and backups

The app does not provide its own cloud service. Device or computer backups may include and later restore app data depending on your settings. Apple handles App Store transactions and may provide purchase reports, analytics, or diagnostics under its own policies and settings. These services are separate from the maintenance content stored locally.

Support

For help or privacy questions, contact Massiveworx at info@massiveworx.com. The support link opens your email app where configured. Information you voluntarily provide to support is used to respond to your request. Please avoid including unnecessary confidential information.

Changes

This statement will be updated if the app's data practices change. Check the revision date above.
